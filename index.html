Structured Learning Modules for Beginners

Simple step-by-step roadmaps designed without complex jargon:

📱 1. Understanding Internet Connectivity

The difference between SIM mobile data balance and local Wi-Fi networks.

  • Mobile Data (4G/5G): Provided by your telecom SIM card with a daily limit (e.g., 1.5 GB/day).
  • Wi-Fi Connection: High-speed internet connected through a home or community router.
  • Hotspot Sharing: How to share data with a family member's phone without wires.
  • Airplane Mode: Quick reset for network connectivity issues.

🌐 2. Web Browsers & Search Engines

Navigating the internet safely without clicking misleading banners.

  • Browser App (Chrome, Edge): The vehicle or door that takes you to websites.
  • Search Engine (Google): The library index that helps you find things when you don't know the exact address.
  • Spotting Ads: Look out for small bold tags saying "Sponsored" or "Ad" at the top of results.
  • Browser Tabs: How to open multiple pages and close background tabs to save phone memory.

🏛️ 3. Accessing Public Utility Services

Using authentic government portals without middleman agent exploitation.

  • DigiLocker: Paperless verified storage for Aadhaar, PAN card, and driving licenses.
  • Electricity & Water Bills: Direct online payments via consumer number on official boards.
  • Train & Bus Booking: Official IRCTC and state transport mobile applications.
  • Check Domain Extensions: Genuine Indian government portals always end in .gov.in or .nic.in.

⚠️ 4. Identifying Common Scams

Critical red flags every first-generation learner must remember.

  • Lottery / KBC Prize Scams: You cannot win a lottery that you never entered.
  • PIN is ONLY for Sending Money: Entering your UPI PIN deducts money from your account, never receives it.
  • Remote Access Apps: Never install apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer on stranger instructions.
  • KYC Expiry Threats: Banks never ask you to update details via random WhatsApp numbers.
